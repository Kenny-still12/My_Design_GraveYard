PulseGrid Technologies

A startup that helps small businesses monitor electricity usage, internet uptime, security cameras, and smart devices from one dashboard.

Think of it like: “IT monitoring + smart building management for small businesses.”

What They Sell
Main Product

A web dashboard where business owners can:

Monitor power usage
Detect internet outages
